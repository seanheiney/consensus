# Code of conduct

This project is a place to argue about ideas, not about people. The panel is adversarial; the people are not.

## What we expect

- Be direct about the work and kind to the person. Critique the code, the claim or the design, with a reason.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you conclude.
- Credit other people's work, and say when you are unsure.
- Keep it on topic in issues, pull requests and discussions.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualized language or imagery, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption. This applies in every project space and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/seanheiney/consensus/security/advisories/new) (it is private, which is why it is used here too) or by contacting @seanheiney on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove content, and warn, suspend or ban anyone whose behavior breaks these rules, in proportion to what happened.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
